Be counted without being named

If the VA foreclosure crisis affected your household, you can add it to the count for your state. You choose what Veterans Foreclosure Crisis (VFC) can share and whether we can reach you.

Compare the three choices · Email to join · Get Help with foreclosure or eviction

Change your choice or leave

Tell VFC anytime, in whatever way works for you. You can leave or move to a more private option on the next site update without giving a reason.

The intake file stays off the website and VFC keeps it private. A public listing includes only the details you specifically agree to share.

What a public listing can reveal

A first name and state can identify you to someone who knows your situation. You choose what goes on the site, such as a separate email, social account, fundraiser, or a statement you wrote. VFC keeps home addresses and phone numbers private. We can remove a listing from this site, but a news story or other outside publication can stay online. How to share your story safely

Talk to a reporter

You can contact NPR’s reporting team directly. Sending the form tells the reporters you’re willing to be contacted. They decide what to follow up on.

If you ask VFC for an introduction, we ask again about each specific request. We keep the household list private, and you can withdraw your permission at any point.

Email VFC to join the count

Email veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com with COUNT in the subject line. In the message, give your state and the number of your choice. You can also tell us in the group chat if you’re already in it.

About This Site & Reuse

Updated in place; corrections are logged publicly. Last updated 2026-11-25.

Compiled and edited by Leann Ledford, MSW graduate student, Washington State Certified Victim Advocate, 2022 Elizabeth Dole Foundation Caregiver Fellow, and caregiver-spouse in one of the documented cases.

Email veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com. Corrections, source requests and press enquiries all reach the same inbox. If you are the family this is happening to, start here.

Independent public-interest project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or the U.S. government. General information and public-interest documentation only; not legal advice.

Reuse: the record, writing, charts, toolkits and data are CC BY 4.0, the site code is MIT. Take it, adapt it, translate it, republish it, including commercially. Credit Veterans Foreclosure Crisis and note the date you took it, so the source and the as-of date travel with the figure.

Funding and organizational disclosure: VFC is independent and family-led, with no institutional funder. Public support links may support directly affected families. If the funding structure changes materially, this disclosure changes with it.

In crisis? The Veterans Crisis Line is 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Around the clock. You do not need to be enrolled in VA care, and you do not have to be in danger right now to call.
