If you are facing foreclosure or eviction

Your information is private.

  • In crisis: Veterans Crisis Line. Call 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Open 24 hours. No VA enrollment needed.
  • Sale date or eviction notice: First 72 hours, a one-page checklist.
  • Help for where you are now: Get Help, free national phone lines by stage.
  • Reach this project: intake form, about 2 minutes. Or email veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com. Do not attach case documents to a first email. What to leave out.
  • Your information is private: nothing about your family is published without your yes. How that works.
  • Other veteran families: VASP Program Family Support and Information, a Facebook group for affected families, co-managed with this project. S289
  • Tell NPR: NPR's survey for veterans pushed into a higher-cost VA loan or facing foreclosure, updated July 30 2026. S288
  • Legal advice: this project is not a law firm and cannot take your case. First 72 hours shows how to find legal aid.

If you want to help, report, or check the record

Email veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com with the subject word shown.

  • Reporters: press desk. Subject: Press.
  • Congressional staff: one-page brief. Subject: Congressional.
  • Attorneys and legal aid: veteran families need help before a foreclosure sale to sort out their options, and after it with post-sale problems and eviction. Some areas have no legal aid with room for these cases, many legal aid offices do not take post-sale cases, and many are not familiar with how VA loans differ from other mortgages. Subject: Legal.
  • Volunteers: intake form, or subject: Volunteer. Useful work: checking citations, reading hearing transcripts, calling congressional offices, spreadsheets.
  • Found an error: name the page and the mistake. Subject: Correction. Fixes are dated on the correction record.
  • Sources and data: source and claims registers and data downloads. Subject: Source request.
  • Updates: Latest lists every change with its date. There is no email list yet. Subject: Updates, to hear when there is one.

What happens after you write

One person reads every message.

  • Reply time: a few days. This project has no staff.
  • Answered first: anyone with a sale date or eviction notice. Put that in the subject line.
  • Published: nothing about a household without that household's yes to that specific thing. How this record is kept.
  • Documents you send: kept private. Deleted when you ask.
