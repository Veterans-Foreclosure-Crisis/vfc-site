Share your story, safely

Adding your experience to the record can help move this, but only on your terms. Here is exactly what we will and won’t publish, and how to send it with the least risk to your household.

What we may publish (with your consent)

  • ✓Your situation described by state and general circumstance (e.g. “a Pacific Northwest caregiver household in active foreclosure”)
  • ✓The documented pattern your experience illustrates
  • ✓Only the minimum detail needed to make the point, and only what you approve

What we will never publish

  • ✗Your name, address, or any contact information
  • ✗Your loan number, court case number, or any agency file number
  • ✗Any reference to a minor child
  • ✗Anything that could let a servicer, court, agency, or your local community recognize your household
  • ✗Any detail about a pending legal matter you ask us to keep private

Minimum-necessary-detail

Before you send anything, ask: does this detail help make the point, or could it identify my household? When in doubt, leave it out. We would rather have a safe, general account than a specific one that puts you at risk.
