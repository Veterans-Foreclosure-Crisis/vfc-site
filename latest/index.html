Latest updates and dated events in the veterans foreclosure crisis

News & record

Every hearing, congressional action, program event, statement, and press item on this crisis, in reverse-chronological order, each with its source. Where a citation is still being pinned, the entry is marked.

November 12: The House passes the modified standalone Major Richard Star Act, H.R. 2102, 392–18. This is a House passage vote, not enactment; the modified text must be checked separately from the original standalone bill and from TCAVA section 101. House Clerk Roll Call 323.

November 11: The House passes TCAVA (H.R. 9237) by voice vote. Its proposed VA home-loan fee and other provisions are not enacted law. The July 210–211 vote concerned a motion to recommit, not final passage. House Clerk official actions. The same day, Rep. Escobar announced the bipartisan Senate S. 5703 Home Loan Navigator companion. Independent counseling is a proposed preventive service, not a post-sale remedy. Escobar release.

November 10: GAO releases its first Partial Claim Program report. GAO finds that VA has not developed a comprehensive evaluation plan; its recommendation remains open. VA expects to begin examining trends and outcomes in December 2027. The report does not determine individual liability or provide restitution to families already displaced. GAO-27-108908 · Read the VFC analysis of the GAO report. Servicer implementation remains due November 28.

November 6: NCLC explains why the new VA Partial Claim still leaves families out. NCLC attorney Steve Sharpe identifies restrictions affecting some borrowers who previously used COVID-era relief, surviving spouses who may need to assume a loan, and homeowners whose existing monthly payment remains unaffordable. Servicers are beginning to offer the new program; that does not verify access or results on any individual loan. This is independent analysis, not an NCLC endorsement of VFC. NCLC source and limitations (S329) · VFC's existing eligibility analysis.

Why it matters: "The VA opened the program" answers only the first question. A family still needs its own servicer to offer it, must meet VA's eligibility rules, and must be able to afford the resulting payment. Prior participation in a government relief program is not proof that a borrower now qualifies. See what to ask your servicer.

October 30: NPR documents higher payments after VA loan modifications. Using data obtained from VA, NPR reports that 63% of the modifications it examined increased borrowers' monthly payments; more than 15,000 increases were at least $150 per month and more than 700 were at least $1,000. These are modification outcomes, not completed foreclosures. The dataset's periods, denominators and comparison with other loan programs require careful reconciliation. NPR source and limitations (S320).

Why it matters: A mortgage modification changes loan terms; it does not necessarily make the payment affordable. A veteran can be recorded as current on a modified loan while paying much more each month. The oversight question is not just how many modifications were approved, but what happened to monthly payments and whether families could keep their homes afterward. The harm has not ended: veteran families still face foreclosure, and some have been offered higher-payment modifications that may be unaffordable. An offered modification is not meaningful protection if the household cannot sustain its payment. These figures do not establish that a particular modification caused a later foreclosure.

DATES TO WATCH

Three federal deadlines affecting veterans facing foreclosure

An agency deadline does not prove a benefit reached a borrower, and a report due date does not confirm the report was delivered.

Nov 10, 2026 · GAO report released. GAO-27-108908 was publicly released ahead of the Nov 12 statutory reporting deadline under Public Law 119-37 §7308. Its evaluation recommendation is open; committee receipt is a separate question. Official GAO report.

Nov 28, 2026 · VA deadline for servicers to fully implement the new loan-relief waterfall and Partial Claim. Implementation does not establish eligibility or completed borrower relief. VA implementation notice · S025.

Dec 11, 2026 · Federal continuing-appropriations deadline and statutory sunset of the Advisory Committee on Homeless Veterans, absent further enactment. Public Law 119-103. Neither date guarantees an agency action.
