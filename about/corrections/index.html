The correction record

Corrections & accountability

When we get something wrong in public, we fix it in public. Every correction to a published fact appears here with its date, what it said before, what it says now, and why it changed, so this record can be trusted the way an outlet's record is trusted.

This record begins with the public announcement of the site. Nothing has been published to an audience yet, so there is nothing yet to correct. That is a statement of fact, not an absence of standards: the standard is that a correction is never quietly edited into place, and never removed once posted.

Working notes written while the site was being built are not corrections and are not kept on this page. They are held in the project archive.

Mechanical fixes are handled differently, and the line is worth stating precisely. Spelling, punctuation, a broken link, a typo that changes nothing about a claim: fixed without a note. Anything that changes what a reader would take away, a figure, a date, a name, a characterisation of what someone said or did, gets a dated entry here, every time.

Retraction is a last resort. Removing part of the record destroys the ability to check what was said and when, which is the opposite of what this site is for. Material is removed only in three circumstances: removal is legally required; the flaws are serious enough that no correction repairs them; or the verification process was not followed before publication. If anything is retracted, this page will say what it was, when it went, and why. The absence will not be silent.

Found something wrong? veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com. Tell us the page and what is wrong, and we will correct it here.
