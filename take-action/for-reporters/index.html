Press desk

For reporters

One page for working journalists. The facts, quotes, and figures live on Evidence and Data & Methods. This page is contact, materials, and ground rules.

Contact

veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com

A veteran- and family-led oversight project. Put “Press” in the subject line and your deadline in the first sentence.

Response expectation

We aim to respond to press inquiries within one business day. Same-day deadlines are met when possible, say so up front. Family witnesses and recorded testimony are available with consent and de-identification.

Interview protocol & ground rules

  • 01On-record scope. Project leads speak on the record about the cohort-level facts, the policy, and the record. Individual family details are on-record only with that family’s consent.
  • 02The minor is never included. No minor child is named, shown, or made identifiable, full firewall policy →
  • 03No case-specific comment during active litigation. We link only what is already public and publish no case, court, or file numbers, full firewall policy →

We publish corrections at /corrections, hold us to it.
