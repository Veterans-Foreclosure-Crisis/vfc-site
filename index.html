The veterans foreclosure crisis

Everyone got the same COVID help. Only veterans got trapped.

Two problems: the VA’s rescue program excludes the families already harmed, and for everyone else it isn’t available until servicers implement it. Until then, veteran families keep losing homes the government told them to pause payments on.

The whole story

1What happened

During COVID, the federal government told homeowners it was safe to pause their mortgage payments. Every other federal loan program kept a way to resume the normal payment afterward, only VA borrowers lost theirs, twice: one administration ended the original exit in October 2022, and the next cancelled its replacement in May 2025 with eight days’ notice, while tens of thousands of eligible families were still in line. Congress passed a bipartisan fix in July 2025, and it took the VA eleven months to open the program. The status bar above is where that stands today.

3What must happen

Pause & count

Pause VA foreclosures and evictions until the new relief is actually working at every servicer, and publish the monthly counts. The VA has used a pause before; members of Congress and the National Consumer Law Center have already asked for it again.

Remove the handbook barriers

The barriers excluding already-harmed families were not written by Congress into H.R. 1815, the VA Home Loan Program Reform Act. VA added them in handbook text and can revise them the same way.

Count the displaced

Publish the monthly VALERI aggregates, new defaults, foreclosure starts, and VA-acquired REO, and build a remediation pathway for families already foreclosed, past sale, or displaced during the implementation gap. The policy is forward-only and leaves that cohort with no remedy. The mandated GAO review arrives after the harm, not during it.

Use the authority already given

Congress expanded the Secretary’s authority in 38 U.S.C. §3737(h) in November 2025. It permits interim guidance and gap-period relief before regulations issue, and it sits unused.

The June 2026 policy is final, but it is not operational at every servicer until November 28, 2026. The problem is implementation, not statute. No new law is required for interim guidance or a remediation pathway. See all six asks →

How the rule changed

Four documents in eleven months. The protection was narrower at the end than at the start.

Jul 30, 2025. Congress creates the partial claim. Bipartisan, signed into law.

Nov 12, 2025. A funding bill narrows the foreclosure protection that came with it. It had stood for 105 days.

Feb to Mar, 2026. VA drafts the rules and takes public comment.

Jun 1, 2026. VA publishes the final. It carries two barriers that were not in the draft anyone commented on.

Jun 15 to Nov 28, 2026. Live at VA is not the same as available at your mortgage company. The two dates are up to 180 days apart.
