Servicer tracker

What this is, and what it is not

VA created the Partial Claim and the loss-mitigation waterfall. Whether an individual mortgage company has put them in place, and whether a particular borrower can actually get one, are two different questions, and during a rollout the answer can vary between companies.

This tracker reports only what VFC can verify from named sources. It is not a compliance grade, not a ranking, and not a performance score.

“No public evidence located” does not mean a servicer has not implemented the programme. It means a bounded search of that company’s public pages on the date shown did not find a page confirming it. The company may well have implemented it without saying so publicly.

Two servicers are listed. That is deliberate: this is a small, evidence-calibrated start, and rows are added only when a source supports them. A company’s absence from this table says nothing about it.

If this is your loan, what to ask

  • Ask your servicer whether it has implemented VA’s current loss-mitigation waterfall, including the VA Partial Claim.
  • Ask which option your loan was evaluated for, and why the others were ruled out.
  • Ask for the decision and its terms in writing.
  • Use the servicer’s official assistance route in the table above, so the request is on their record.

For the wider steps, and for what to do in the first days after a notice, see the first 72 hours and get help. For the policy record behind the Partial Claim, see the policy record.

How this page is maintained

Every row above is generated at build time from servicers.csv and servicer_evidence.csv, with each source ID resolved against the site’s existing source registry. Servicer-specific status and evidence rows are not hand-maintained in this page, and a reference to a servicer or source that does not exist fails the build rather than publishing.

Status values are recorded as evidence states, not verdicts. There is no “not implemented” value, because establishing that would need a source affirmatively showing nonimplementation for a stated date and scope.
