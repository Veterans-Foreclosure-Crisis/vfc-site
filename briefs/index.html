THE BRIEFS

The same record, written for five different desks.

One to three pages each, print ready, every figure sourced and dated. Take the one that matches the desk you are walking into. Nothing is asserted in a brief that is not already on this site.

These exist to be handed on. Print them, attach them to an email, leave one on a desk. You do not need to credit this site and you do not need permission.
