Contact: veteransforeclosurecrisis@gmail.com. Corrections, source requests and press enquiries all reach the same inbox. If you are the family this is happening to, start here.

Follow this record: Substack. Documents or tips: the same inbox above.

In crisis? The Veterans Crisis Line is 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Around the clock. You do not need to be enrolled in VA care, and you do not have to be in danger right now to call.
